# Sparkweave branding asset provenance

The current logo and mod-family banner were copied on 18 October 2026 from the
shared Sparkweave branding kit, version 2.0, at
`E:\Repos\esm-loader\docs\branding`. Their filenames, source version, roles,
origins and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `assets/manifest.json` and checked
by `BUILD.ps1`.

The logo and banner are original Sparkweave identity artwork generated for this
project. They do not use official Enshrouded marks or imply endorsement. These
files are included only in the source archive and documentation; they are not
runtime map, terrain, frame or marker assets. The original Minimap artwork,
upstream credits and MIT license remain unchanged.

`esm-logo-dark-v1.png` is retained only as a historical ESM identity reference.
Its hash and historical status remain recorded in `provenance.json`.
